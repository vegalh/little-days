🍂

Nature color hunt

Outdoor · 20–30 min · Ages 2–5

What you'll need

  • A small basket or bag
  • Shoes and clothes for the weather

Steps

  1. Pick three colors to look for together.
  2. Walk slowly and collect one thing of each color.
  3. At home, line them up and compare shapes and textures.

What your child practices

  • Observation
  • Color words and language
  • Sorting and matching
